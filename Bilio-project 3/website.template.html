Bilio is a technical soft goods
design laboratory.

We partner with the world’s most
innovative and exacting companies
to envision, invent, fabricate, and
manufacture exceptional products.

We think materials first, letting
the inherent characteristics of
each material inform our ideas.

We work with clients who share
our curiosity, sensibilities,
and perfectionism; and appreciate
our idiosyncrasies.

Our partnerships span decades,
seasons, and product cycles, and
we approach each relationship
with the utmost discretion.

Our practice is located
in Berkeley, California.
